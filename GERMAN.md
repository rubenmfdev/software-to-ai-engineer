# German: B1 → B2

I live in Zurich and many AI and engineering postings here ask for good German, so B2 is the goal. It also puts a recognised certificate on my CV.

**Budget:** about 4 hours a week alongside the roadmap. Reaching B2 from B1 typically takes a few hundred hours, so this is a 9–12 month track, not a 24-week one.

## Weekly routine (~4h)

| What | Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Duolingo | 10–15 min a day (~1.5h) | Keeps the streak and vocabulary. It doesn't build B2 speaking or writing, so it's the warm-up, not the plan |
| Coursebooks | 1h | Finish the B1 book properly, especially grammar I skipped. Use the A2 book only to look things up |
| Listening | 1h | Podcasts and slow news (below), on commutes |
| Speaking or writing | 30 min | One short text a week, or a conversation. This is the skill that improves least on its own |

## Resources

**Already have**
- Duolingo
- A2 and B1 coursebooks

**Free**
- DW [Nicos Weg](https://learngerman.dw.com/en/nicos-weg/c-36519789): free video course from A1 to B1. Use the B1 part to review and find gaps
- [Easy German podcast](https://www.easygerman.org/podcast): real conversations, roughly B1 and up
- Deutsche Welle *Langsam gesprochene Nachrichten*: slow news, B2-level listening
- Goethe-Institut sample exams (Übungssätze) for the B2 exam, on goethe.de

**Paid courses in Zurich (check current dates and prices)**
- ECAP Zurich: [B1 evening course](https://www.ecap.ch/en/courses/98056/evening-german/), Tue/Thu 18:30–20:15, 27 Oct 2026 – 4 Feb 2027, CHF 630
- ECAP: [online B2 evening course](https://www.ecap.ch/en/courses/96713/german-in-the-evening/) with telc B2 exam prep
- [Klubschule Migros](https://www.klubschule.ch) and [EB Zürich](https://www.eb-zuerich.ch): evening and exam-prep courses
- City of Zurich subsidised German courses for residents (run by ECAP): [overview](https://www.ecap.ch/en/locations/zurich/german-language-courses-of-the-city-of-zurich/)

## Milestones

- [ ] Finish the B1 coursebook, including all grammar chapters
- [ ] Finish the Nicos Weg B1 review
- [ ] Take a free B2 sample exam to get a baseline
- [ ] Enrol in a B2 course (evening or online)
- [ ] Book the exam: **telc Deutsch B2** or **Goethe-Zertifikat B2**
- [ ] Pass B2

## Tie it to the job search

- Read German-language tech job ads each week and note the vocabulary.
- Prepare a 2-minute self-introduction in German for interviews.
- Swiss German is a different spoken language from the Standard German taught in courses. Learn to understand it a little, but take the exam in Standard German.
